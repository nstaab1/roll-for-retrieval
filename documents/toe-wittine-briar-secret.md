# Elder Briar's secret

Briar has a sister. Her name is Hazel, and she left Cinder Ward forty years ago after the two of them fell out over their father's orchard. She went down to the lowland towns and never came back. Most of the town thinks Briar was an only child, and he has never corrected them.

Every winter Briar writes Hazel a letter and never sends it. There are forty of them now, in a cedar box under his bed, tied with the same red cord. He doesn't know if she's alive.

How it plays at the table: if a pilgrim asks Briar about family, he talks about the weather instead. If someone finds the box, the top letter ends "The orchard came in thin again this year. I kept the best apples for you anyway." If the party ever meets an old woman who taps the table twice before she speaks, the way Briar does, that's Hazel. She'll ask whether her brother is still stubborn.
