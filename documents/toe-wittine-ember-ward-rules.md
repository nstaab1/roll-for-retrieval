# The rules of the ember wards

Written by Elder Briar of Cinder Ward, for the chosen who carry the ember wards up the mountain. Keep this with your ward. Read it again when the path gets hard, because it will.

Only the chosen carry a ward. Each ward is lit by my hand from the ward fire, on the night of the ceremony. A lantern lit any other way is not an ember ward, and the mountain will not honor it. If you meet someone on the path carrying a lantern that wasn't lit in Cinder Ward, do not trust it to light your way.

A ward holds several flames, not one. Fear, doubt and the mountain's trials put them out one at a time, and you will feel each one go. While even one flame burns, the ward fire's warmth goes with you. Keep your ward out of water and away from the hands in the fog.

A dark flame can come back. Rest where the old pilgrims rested, and honor the dead you find on the path, and your ward will take a flame again. Never feed a ward from a campfire, a torch or a spell. A borrowed flame burns out before the summit, and it takes the ward's own flames with it.

Keep to the ember path. The outposts and keeps along it were built for pilgrims, and most are empty now. Shelter in them, but don't trust what you find inside them.

You walk together. Nobody climbs ahead and nobody is left behind. You rise together or you fall together.

At the altar fire, set every ward into the flame at the same moment. A ward set in alone gives its light to nothing, and the fire stays low. Wait for each other, even if one of you arrives with a single flame left.

Flicker goes with you. Protect him as you would protect one another.
