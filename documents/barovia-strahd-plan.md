# Strahd's plan for the town after the festival

The festival went the way Strahd wanted. The cultists in the crowd answer to him. They packed the wicker sun with powder and shot down whoever tried to take charge in the square. By the end of the night the Baron, Izek and Lady Wachter were all dead, and the guards had dropped their weapons. Nobody runs the town now, and that was the point.

What he does next: he waits three days. The town has no Burgomaster, no enforcer and no rival lady with her own guards, and people get frightened enough to listen to anyone. On the fourth night a black carriage stops outside the church. Its driver carries a letter offering the town Strahd's protection, and order in the streets, if the town hands over Ireena.

The secret the party doesn't know: Strahd means the letter for them, not the town. A second letter in the carriage invites the whole party to dinner at the castle. He wants to see the people who walked out of his festival alive, and he'll offer them the town to rule if they'll hold it for him.

If the party burns the first letter in front of the crowd, the cultists still hiding in town strike the church the next night.
