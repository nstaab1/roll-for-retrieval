# Prep for next night: the morning after the festival

Open in the town square at dawn. Smoke is still coming off what's left of the wicker sun. Nobody has cleared the bodies. The Baron, Izek and Lady Wachter are dead, the cultists who lived are hiding, and the guards who surrendered don't know who they report to.

Scenes to have ready:

- The square. Guards picking through the wreckage. One of them recognizes the party and asks loudly whether they were in on it. Let the crowd decide what it thinks of them.
- The church. Father Lucian has burns on his hands and arms and won't leave Jeska's side. He wants the party to help carry the dead inside, because the church is still hallowed ground. If they help, he asks one of them to speak at the funeral, since nobody else in town will.
- The Blue Water Inn. Danica is cold with them, colder than last time. Erwin wants to know what happened to the were-raven Lady Wachter held in the square.
- The Wachter house. The doors are shut and her guards are arguing in the yard over whether they still owe her house anything.

Things to decide before the night: which surrendered guard tries to take charge first, and whether the party backs him. Bring the cultist names from last time, because the ones who got away are still in town.
