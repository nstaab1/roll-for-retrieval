# Street encounters in town after the festival

Roll a d8 whenever the party crosses town, or when a scene drags.

1. A guard patrol of four stops the party and asks where they stood when the wicker sun went off. They're scared, not hostile, unless someone mentions the sewer.
2. Two cultists in commoners' clothes, still wearing their cloaks underneath. They split up and run if spotted. One of them carries a list of names from the town, with the Baron, Izek and Lady Wachter crossed out.
3. A mourner carrying a child's festival toy asks the party to take it to Father Lucian at the church.
4. A raven lands on the nearest roof and watches the party for a full minute before flying toward the Blue Water Inn.
5. A surrendered guard nails a notice to a door. The town gates close at dusk by order of "whoever is in charge."
6. A crowd outside the Burgomaster's mansion argues over who's in charge now. Roll again to see who turns up to answer them.
7. Burned scraps of the wicker sun blow down the street. One has a powder keg's stamp on it.
8. Nothing. The streets are empty and every shutter is closed.
