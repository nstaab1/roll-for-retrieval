3 Turn one knob

Change one value only, so you know which one fixed it.

  • It's in the list, past your RERANK_DEPTH. Search found it and the reranker never read it. Raise RERANK_DEPTH in src/choices.ts, as far as your RETRIEVAL_K. Nothing needs rebuilding.
  • It isn't in the list. The reranker only sorts what search returns, so a deeper rerank can't reach it. Change CHUNK_SIZE in src/choices.ts to another size from Act 2's list: 300, 600, 900, 1200. Then rebuild your index. 
npm run ingest

The rebuild takes about a minute and costs about $0.003. A chunk's vector stands for all of its text at once. At one size, the fire shares a chunk with talk about other things, and the vector lands somewhere in between. At another size the cuts fall elsewhere, and the chunk that holds the fire can read more like the question.
