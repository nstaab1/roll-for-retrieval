Roll for Retrieval

One pipeline, built act by act. You turn the audio of two real D&D campaigns into text, search it, answer questions with citations, then break what you built and fix it. Start at Act 0 and follow the pages in order. Each act has its own page, and the side quests sit just before the act that uses them.
